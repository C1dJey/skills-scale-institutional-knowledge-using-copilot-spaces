# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository centralizes the project management guidance used by OctoAcme so the team can align on how work is initiated, planned, executed, and improved. The process starts with project initiation, where the team validates the business problem, identifies stakeholders, defines measurable success criteria, and produces a lightweight project one-pager. Once the case is approved, planning turns that idea into a backlog with priorities, milestones, estimates, dependencies, and a clear Definition of Done so work can move from concept to execution with shared expectations.

During execution, OctoAcme relies on defined roles and delivery rhythms to keep work moving. Product Managers define what should be built and measure outcomes, Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communication, Developers implement and validate the work, and QA/testing ensures quality and acceptance against the agreed criteria. Cross-functional teams use regular standups, milestone reviews, demos, and weekly PM/PdM check-ins to maintain visibility, remove blockers, and escalate issues when dependencies or business impact require it.

Risk management and communication are built into the workflow rather than treated as separate activities. The team maintains a risk register, reviews risks and dependencies in recurring syncs, and follows clear escalation paths from team triage to the Product Lead and sponsor when a blocker is significant. Quality is reinforced through testing gates, CI checks, smoke tests, and release readiness reviews, with security scanning and manual QA used when appropriate. The lifecycle closes with release verification and retrospectives that capture lessons learned and convert them into concrete action items, strengthening the team's continuous improvement process.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme’s approach follows a practical lifecycle: initiate the work, plan the delivery, execute in small iterations, release with verification, and close with a retrospective. This keeps the team focused on customer value, clear ownership, and measurable outcomes while maintaining strong communication and quality controls throughout the project.
